Kirscher et al., Rethinking Post-Hoc Calibration in Semantic Segmentation, appears in the TMLR official October2026 accepted list (https://www.jmlr.org/tmlr/papers/). Its translation-invariant and decision-preserving calibrators, aerial-road evaluation and label-flip benefit/harm audit are relevant precedents. Method reading used arXiv2607.01902v2; official bibliography uses the journal's author order (Rokuss before Kovacs). This project instead studies restricted background candidates and empirical source quantile thresholds without claiming improved probability calibration. The distinction does not establish performance superiority or broad novelty.
